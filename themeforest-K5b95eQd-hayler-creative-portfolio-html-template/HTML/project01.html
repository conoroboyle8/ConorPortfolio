Fantasia For Strings & Harp

Overview:

Forces
String orchestra and harp

Recording
European Recording Orchestra

A richly lyrical orchestral work that reimagines the expressive colours of the Romantic orchestra through a contemporary lens.

A wonderful piece, full of colour, lyricism, harmony, and with his individual stamp clearly on the music.

The piece begins in suspended stillness before fragments of melody surface within gently shifting harmonies.

The harp acts both as colouristic partner and subtle structural guide, illuminating the harmonic landscape as the music opens out.
