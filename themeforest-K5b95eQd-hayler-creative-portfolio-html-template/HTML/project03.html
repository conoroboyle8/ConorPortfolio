Project:

Platonic Shapes

Overview:

Platonic Shapes is Conor O'Boyle's popular music project, built around song-led releases, playful production, visual artwork, and a more immediate sound-world than his composing work for screen and concert hall.

Project
Platonic Shapes

Focus
Song-led releases, production, artwork

Artist
Conor O'Boyle

The project sits inside the wider portfolio as a place for independent releases, visual identity, pop-facing musical experiments, and a more direct production voice.

Platonic Shapes gives the portfolio a second register: more colourful, immediate, graphic, and song-led than the screen and concert work.

The artwork has its own grammar of geometry, saturated colour, collage, and playful distortion, which can become a strong visual counterweight within the wider composer site.

A vivid side-channel for songs, artwork and odd little worlds.

The project does not need to sit apart anymore. It can work as a recurring visual and musical thread through the portfolio.

In this template, Platonic Shapes can do a lot of the colour work while Conor's composer pages remain cinematic and restrained.
